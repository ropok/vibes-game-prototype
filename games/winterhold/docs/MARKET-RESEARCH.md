# Reference research and prototype positioning

Research checked 16 October 2026. These are design observations from primary product sources, not evidence of demand, revenue, or a validated audience.

| Reference | Source observation | Winterhold hypothesis to test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Kingdom Two Crowns](https://kingdomthegame.com/kingdom-two-crowns/) | Its official description combines side-scrolling micro strategy, exploration, recruitment, building, and defense with a minimalist presentation. | Moving a visible leader through the world can make settlement choices understandable without a dense management screen. |
| [Thronefall official store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2239150/Thronefall/) | The developer describes a minimalist kingdom game that alternates building and defense, with troops and direct player combat. | A complete short defense session with a few legible purchases is a useful prototype scope. |
| [Dome Keeper](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1637320/Dome_Keeper/) | Its official store description connects gathering resources and upgrading equipment with returning in time to defend against waves. | A visible wave timer can give gathering choices urgency and make preparation feel consequential. |

Winterhold explores a winter frontier settlement with a visible captain, nearby context actions, resource gathering, structures, recruits, trained defenders, and a finite defense objective. It should be evaluated on whether players understand where to go and why to spend resources before the first wave. Names, artwork, code, and setting should be original; these references supply genre context only.

The [Thronefall demo announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/games/2239150/announcements/detail/3698065064765651542?l=english) was also checked, but did not expose readable body text in this research pass; the feature observation above comes from its official store page.

## Validation still needed

- Observe at least five first-time players without explaining controls beyond the game itself.
- Record time to first gather, first structure, recruit, and trained defender; note where players stall.
- Ask whether movement adds exploration value or merely slows repeated purchases.
- Compare wave readability, pacing, and restart intent across at least two sessions per player.
- Collect device and input method, session completion, confusion points, and voluntary replay interest. Avoid projecting small playtests into sales estimates.
