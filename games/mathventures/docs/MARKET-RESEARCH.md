# Arithmetic battle references

Reviewed public creator descriptions on 16 October 2026. These examples establish comparable mechanics, not market size, revenue, learning outcomes or demand.

| Reference | Observed mechanic | Prototype implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Mathleticus](https://featurekreep.itch.io/mathleticus) | Alternates combining numbers into a target for attack and solving an equation under pressure for defense. Browser playable. | Math can drive the combat action itself; distinct attack/defense phases give problems context. |
| [MathCombat](https://particleatonstudio.itch.io/mathcombat) | Addition battles, arrow-key answer selection, pixel art; creator describes ages 6–10 and prototype status. | Four clear answers offer a low-friction entry. Touch targets should preserve the same clarity on phones. |
| [Battle Math](https://leftturnworkshop.itch.io/battle-math) | Arithmetic powers attacks/healing and defense; typed answers, configurable factor ranges, and correction after mistakes. Windows download. | Difficulty controls and showing the correct result support practice. Avoid assuming every comparable game's theme fits children. |

Working hypothesis: a short, touch-friendly fantasy battle with immediate arithmetic feedback can be a useful prototype to test. This is a design hypothesis, not a validated commercial claim. Test it with intended players: can they start unaided, understand why damage occurs, recover from mistakes, and choose another round? Record completion, confusion and feedback before expanding content or claiming educational effectiveness.

All mechanics above come from creator page descriptions; no assets or text from those games are reused.
